The GEI Method

Observation

Every investigation begins with observation. Observe the words. Observe the sequence. Observe the relationships. Before conclusions are reached, patterns must first be identified.

Pattern Recognition

Systems often reveal recurring structures. Examples: • Collection • Storage • Regulation • Release Recognizing patterns is an important step in comparative analysis.

Framework Construction

A framework organizes observations. Instead of isolated facts, a framework seeks connections between multiple observations.

Evidence Evaluation

A useful investigation asks: What evidence supports the observation? What evidence challenges it? What additional information is needed?
