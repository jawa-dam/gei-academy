What Is GEI?

Genesis Engineered Interpretations (GEI) is a framework that explores Genesis through language, symbolism, engineering concepts, and structured observation. Rather than asking only what a passage says, GEI also asks what processes, systems, and patterns may be represented.

Why Study Genesis This Way?

Throughout history people have interpreted Genesis through many lenses: • Historical • Theological • Literary • Scientific GEI introduces an engineering lens, encouraging students to investigate water systems, regulation, containment, flow, storage, and mechanical processes.
