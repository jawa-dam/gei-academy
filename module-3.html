Water Engineering Fundamentals

What Is A Tributary?

A tributary is a stream or river that feeds water into a larger river, lake, or reservoir. Tributaries are collection systems. They gather water from many locations and move it toward storage.

What Is A Reservoir?

A reservoir is a body of stored water. Reservoirs help regulate flow, provide water storage, and create hydraulic pressure.

What Is A Dam?

A dam is a structure that controls the movement of water. Dams can: • Store water • Control flooding • Regulate flow • Generate energy

What Is A Spillway?

A spillway safely releases excess water. Without spillways, water pressure may become dangerous. Spillways act as controlled release systems.

What Is A Waterwheel?

A waterwheel converts flowing water into mechanical movement. Flowing water creates rotation. Rotation creates work.
