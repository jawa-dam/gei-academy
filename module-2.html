The Language Toolbox

Literal Meaning

A literal meaning is the direct meaning of words. Example: "The river is flowing." The sentence describes an actual river.

Figurative Meaning

Figurative language uses words in a symbolic or non-literal way. Example: "He is drowning in work." No actual water is involved.

Metaphors

A metaphor compares one thing to another. Example: "The classroom was a zoo." This does not mean animals were present. It suggests disorder and noise.

Similes

A simile compares using "like" or "as." Example: "The reservoir was as calm as glass."

Anthropomorphism

Anthropomorphism gives human characteristics to non-human things. Example: "The river swallowed the bridge." The river is described as performing a human action.

Homophones

Homophones sound alike but have different meanings. Examples: Sea / See Right / Write Their / There
